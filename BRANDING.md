# Gojju Linux brand

## Name
**Gojju Linux** (short form: **gojju**). Named after *tomato gojju*, the tangy, spicy Karnataka dish: simple ingredients, made at home, better than it has any right to be. That is the spirit: a small, homemade project that takes hardware nobody serves yet and makes something good of it.

Not affiliated with Apple, Asahi Linux, Gravity Linux or Omarchy.

> Before publishing: search for existing projects named "gojju", and check the domain and trademark situation.

## Tagline
**Linux, native on Apple Silicon.**

Playful alternates: "Tangy, spicy, homemade." / "Cooked at home on M4."

## Voice
Plain, warm, builder-to-builder, with a little flavour. Never claim hardware works until it has been verified on real hardware. `works`, `untested` and `broken` are the only three states we use.

## Type
Headings: a friendly geometric sans (Inter or system UI). Code and terminal: JetBrains Mono or system monospace.

## Logo
`assets/logo.svg`: a tomato with a green calyx, holding a terminal prompt (`>_`). Use on the dark background. Keep clear space equal to the cursor height. Don't recolour, rotate or add effects.

## Hardware status badges
Worded exactly:
- `works`: verified on real hardware, with the date and kernel version
- `untested`: should work in theory, nobody has tried
- `broken`: tried and failed, link the issue

## Credit and attribution
Gojju builds on others' work. Keep every upstream licence and credit (see CREDITS.md). Don't use the Asahi Linux, Gravity Linux or Omarchy names or logos as ours.
